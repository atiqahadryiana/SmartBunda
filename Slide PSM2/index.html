Introduction

A caring companion for every stage of pregnancy.

Pregnancy changes a mother's nutritional needs daily — yet tracking them is tedious and easy to neglect. SmartBunda turns nutrition into a calm, guided experience: snap a photo of your meal, understand what your body needs, and stay connected to the professionals who care for you.

  • For mothers — effortless meal logging and friendly, trustworthy guidance.
  • For clinicians — doctors, dietitians & nurses monitor patients and record vitals.
  • Localised — built around Malaysian dishes and maternal-health guidelines.

Our mission

To make every pregnancy healthier by putting personalised, culturally-relevant nutrition guidance in every mother's hand — and connecting it to the people who care for her.

Our vision

A future where no mother is left guessing about nutrition — where technology and clinical care work together for confident, well-nourished pregnancies.

The Problem

Healthy eating in pregnancy is harder than it should be.

Mothers want to do right by their babies — but the tools to help them are generic, manual, and disconnected from real clinical care.

Guesswork & generic advice

Most apps aren't built for pregnancy or local cuisine, so mothers second-guess whether their meals meet daily needs like iron and folate.

Tedious manual logging

Typing every dish and portion by hand is slow and discouraging — so food diaries get abandoned within days.

Disconnected from care

Nutrition data lives in one silo and clinical records in another, so doctors and dietitians can't act on what a mother actually eats.

Our Solution

Snap. Understand. Stay on track.

SmartBunda combines an on-device AI food model with a cloud vision assistant — recognising the meal instantly, then turning it into clear, personalised nutrition guidance.

1

Capture the meal

Take a photo or upload one — quality is checked automatically.

2

AI recognises the food

An on-device YOLOv8 model identifies it instantly; Google Gemini handles the long tail.

3

Confirm & log

Pick the portion with a tap, and the meal is saved with full nutrition facts.

Why it's different

A “smart split” that's fast and accurate.

The on-device model answers common dishes in milliseconds — offline and free. When it isn't sure, the scan escalates to a cloud vision model that knows the full range of Malaysian cuisine. Mothers get speed; accuracy never suffers.

Roadmap

From idea to impact.

The complete SmartBunda development journey — from foundations through an AI-powered scanner and connected clinical care, to what's next.

Phase 1 · Foundation

Core app & secure access

The groundwork: a cross-platform Flutter app with role-based accounts for every actor.

  • Email & Google sign-in with email verification
  • Five roles — patient, doctor, dietitian, nurse, admin
  • Patient-only self-registration; staff approved by admin
  • Malaysian food database & clean manual meal logging
Completed
Phase 2 · Nutrition Engine

Personalised tracking & guidance

Turning logged meals into meaningful, pregnancy-aware nutrition insight.

  • Daily targets for calories, protein, iron & folate
  • Portion-aware nutrition facts from the MyFCD database
  • Hydration tracking and a personal health record
  • Gentle meal-log & hydration reminders (8am · 1pm · 8pm)
Completed
Phase 3 · AI Meal Scanner

The “smart split” recognition

The flagship feature: instant, accurate food recognition trained on local cuisine.

  • On-device YOLOv8 model — 172 Malaysian dishes, runs offline
  • Google Gemini cloud vision for the long tail & multi-food plates
  • Confidence-gated escalation: fast when sure, accurate when not
  • Image-quality checks & tap-to-confirm portion icons
Completed
Phase 4 · Connected Care

Clinical monitoring & alerts

Closing the loop between a mother's diet at home and her care team in the clinic.

  • Doctor, dietitian & nurse dashboards
  • Per-appointment vitals recording (weight, Hb, BP)
  • Nutrition plans, consultation notes & appointments
  • Smart alerts & in-app notifications for at-risk mothers
Completed
Phase 5 · Hardening & Polish

Security, quality & experience

Making SmartBunda production-ready — secure, accessible, and refined.

  • Server-side Firestore security rules & per-screen access guards
  • Full QA audit: navigation, validation & action feedback
  • Polished Material 3 design with a complete dark mode
  • Bilingual interface (English / Bahasa Melayu)
In Progress
Phase 6 · The Future

Detection, insights & scale

Where SmartBunda is headed beyond the final-year project.

  • Multi-food bounding-box detection for full plates
  • Trend analytics & clinic-wide reporting dashboards
  • Wearable & appointment-system integrations
  • Expanded dish coverage across the region
Planned
